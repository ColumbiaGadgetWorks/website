Some tools need a short training before you use them on your own. Book it here: we check whether you are a member (members pay half), make sure your waiver is on file, then you pick a time and pay to hold your seat.

{{< training-book >}}

## How the money is used

Half of every training fee goes to the zone that keeps the tool running, for supplies and repairs; the other half is credited to your trainer toward their dues.
