## How the class went

Members were trained in both of the soldering methods the shop uses, and everyone was given a free soldering kit to practice on and take home.

- **Through-hole soldering**, where component legs pass through holes in the board and are soldered on the far side. It is the method most people learn first, it is forgiving, and it is what you want for repairs and for anything that has to survive being knocked about.
- **Reflow and surface-mount**, where much smaller components sit on top of the board on pads of solder paste, and the whole board is heated until the paste melts and pulls each part into place. It is how nearly all modern electronics are built.

Knowing both is the difference between being able to fix the electronics you own and only being able to look at them.
