Somebody at this shop decided we needed an arena, so we built one. The **Chamber of Robotic Aggression** is a member-built battle box: a welded steel frame, a plywood floor, polycarbonate walls, expanded metal over the top, and hazard striping along the bottom rail so nobody forgets what it is for.

## It uses most of the shop

A combat robot is a good excuse to touch nearly every zone in the building, which is part of why we like them:

- The chassis and armor get cut and welded in [metalworking](/tools/welding/), or machined on the [Bridgeport and the Clausing](/tools/machine-shop/) when a part has to be right.
- Wedges, wheel hubs, and mounts come off the [3D printers](/tools/3d-printers/).
- Drive electronics, radio gear, and battery wiring happen at the [electronics bench](/tools/electronics-bench/).
- The arena itself came out of [woodworking](/tools/woodshop/) and the welding bay.

## Want to build one?

Start small. An antweight class bot is cheap, fits on a bench, and teaches you the same lessons a heavier one would, just without the shrapnel. Come to [Open Hack Night](/visit/) on a Thursday and say you want to build a battlebot; there are several members who will immediately have opinions about drive trains.

Build notes and arena details go on the [member wiki](https://wiki.comogadget.casa/), and match nights get announced on [Discord](https://discord.gg/F7kM7ardMs) and the [events calendar](/events/).
