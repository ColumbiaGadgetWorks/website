Columbia Gadget Works is running **free classes on the first Tuesday of every month** for the next 12 months, from November 3, 2026 through October 5, 2027.

The first class, on **Tuesday, November 3, 6 to 8 PM**, is CAD. Two instructors will show you how to model in 3D using modern design software. The class is open to the public, at 1404 Grand Ave, Columbia, MO. More information is coming soon on the [calendar](/calendar/).

The picture is a 3D model of the shop floor with our new zones in color.

If you have an idea for a future class, send it to classes@columbiagadgetworks.org.

Financial assistance for this program has been provided by the City of Columbia Office of Cultural Affairs.
