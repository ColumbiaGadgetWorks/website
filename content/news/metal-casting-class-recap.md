Thanks to everyone who showed up for the metal casting class! We made plaster molds from 3D-printed patterns and poured them in metal, and everyone went home with something they cast themselves.

If you have any ideas for future classes or something to cast in metal, join the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/F7kM7ardMs) or send an email to [mail@columbiagadgetworks.org](mailto:mail@columbiagadgetworks.org).
