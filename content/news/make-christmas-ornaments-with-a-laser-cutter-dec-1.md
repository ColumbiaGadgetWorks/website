Our laser cutter is getting into the holiday spirit, and you can join in.

**Make Ornaments with a Laser Cutter** is a free, all-ages class on **Tuesday, December 1, 6 to 8 pm** at Columbia Gadget Works, 1404 Grand Ave. You'll design a personalized Christmas ornament and watch our [laser cutter](/tools/laser-cutter/) engrave it. Along the way you'll learn how laser cutting works and how to use it safely. Each person takes home up to two ornaments.

Materials and financial support are provided by the City of Columbia Office of Cultural Affairs. There are 20 seats.

[Sign up on the classes page](/classes/) or see everything coming up on the [calendar](/calendar/).
