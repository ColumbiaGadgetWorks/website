title: Membership agreement
description: The Columbia Gadget Works membership agreement, signed by every member.
url: /legal/agreement/
---
As a member of Columbia Gadget Works I agree to:

- Follow the shop rules and the code of conduct.
- Clean up after myself and leave tools and benches ready for the next person.
- Report damage or unsafe conditions right away.
- Be responsible for any guests I bring.
- Keep my dues current. I can cancel at any time.

I understand that membership and badge access can be suspended for unsafe behavior or unpaid dues.
