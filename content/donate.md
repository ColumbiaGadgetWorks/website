## What your donation does

Columbia Gadget Works is run entirely by volunteers, so every dollar goes into the shop and its programs:

- **Keeps Open Hack Night free.** Every Thursday, anyone in Columbia can walk in, learn a tool, and get help with a project at no charge. Rent and utilities for those evenings are paid by donors and members.
- **Buys and maintains equipment.** Laser tubes wear out, end mills break, 3D printers need nozzles and filament. Larger gifts have bought the machines themselves: our laser cutter, CNC router, and 3D printers were all funded by members and donors.
- **Funds free classes.** Soldering, metal casting, machining, and electronics classes are offered free or at cost. Donations cover materials so a class doesn't have to turn anyone away.
- **Supports community projects.** Like the open-source Roll-to-Prone hospital bed our members built in 2020, and outreach at events like Art in the Park.

## Tax-deductible giving

Columbia Gadget Works is a registered **501(c)(3) public charity**. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

- **Legal name:** Columbia Gadget Works
- **EIN:** 27-3926809
- **Address:** 1404 Grand Ave, Columbia, MO 65203

You'll receive an emailed receipt for every online gift. For gifts of $250 or more we'll also send a written acknowledgment for your records.

## Other ways to give

- **By check:** payable to *Columbia Gadget Works*, mailed or dropped off at 1404 Grand Ave, Columbia, MO 65203.
- **Employer matching:** many employers match charitable gifts. Search for us by EIN 27-3926809 in your company's matching portal, or [contact us](/contact/) for any paperwork.
- **Equipment and materials:** we accept working tools, machines, electronics, and shop materials that fit our zones. Please [ask first](/contact/) so we can confirm we have space and a use for it. In-kind donations are acknowledged in writing.
- **Your time:** teach a class, lead a zone, or help on a Thursday. See [classes](/classes/#teach) or just show up.

## Become a member instead

If you'd like to use the shop yourself, [membership](/membership/) is the best way to support us. Dues give you 24/7 access, a project locker, and a vote in how the space is run, and they're the backbone of our budget.
