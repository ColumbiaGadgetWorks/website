Pay after your trainer has signed you off. Pick the tool and who trained you; the fee and the zone fill in. Then pay with Givebutter using **the same email you type here**, so we can record your training.

{{< training-pay >}}

Half of every training fee goes to the zone's budget for upkeep and supplies; the other half is credited to your trainer toward their dues. Once paid, you can [check what you are trained on](/training/).

Is a tool missing, or your trainer not listed? Trainers and fees are set by each zone boss. Ask them, or [contact us](/contact/).
