The shop is organized into **zones**, each looked after by a volunteer lead who keeps it running, keeps it safe, and decides what training its tools require. Members can use any tool they have been trained on, 24/7. Visitors can try the basic tools on Thursday nights and get a demo of anything else. Detailed operating guides, settings, and maintenance logs live on the [member wiki](https://wiki.comogadget.casa/).

**Training:** tools marked *training required* need a sign-off from a zone lead before you use them alone. Training is discounted for members and usually takes an hour or less.
