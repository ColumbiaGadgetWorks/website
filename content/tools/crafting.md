Not everything worth making is made of steel. The crafting zone is the shop's general-purpose making space: a sewing machine, a vinyl cutter, paint and finishing supplies, glues, hand tools, and clear bench space to spread out on. It is the least intimidating corner of the building and a good place to start if the machine noise elsewhere is not your thing yet.

**What people do here:** sew bags, costumes, and repairs, cut vinyl for stickers and shirts, paint and finish parts that came off the [3D printers](/tools/3d-printers/) or the [laser cutter](/tools/laser-cutter/), assemble kits, and work on anything that needs a clean flat surface and good light.

## It pairs with everything else

The crafting zone is where a lot of projects actually get finished. A laser-cut box gets sanded and stained here. A printed cosplay piece gets filled, primed, and painted here. A cut-and-sew project starts on the [CNC router](/tools/cnc-router/) or the laser as a pattern and ends up on the sewing machine. If you are making something that crosses zones, this is usually the last stop.

## Using the zone

No training required. A member will show you where things live and how the sewing machine and vinyl cutter are set up. Please:

- Bring your own fabric, vinyl, and consumables for anything substantial. Shop stock is for small jobs and trying things out.
- Clean the cutting mat and bench when you are done, and put blades and needles in the sharps bin, not the trash.
- Label and shelve work in progress so the bench stays usable for the next person.

Project notes and machine settings go on the [member wiki](https://wiki.comogadget.casa/).
