Columbia Gadget Works is a member-run nonprofit. The **bylaws** set out members' rights, how the board is elected, and how votes work; only the members can change them. The **policies** cover day-to-day matters like dues, safety, zones, and money; the board adopts them after members have had a week to comment.

The official text lives in a public repository on GitHub, and these pages are built straight from it, so what you read here is always the current text. Any member can propose a change there; [How we decide](/policies/how-we-decide/) explains how.
