## What members get

- **24/7 access** to the shop with your own door credential.
- **A storage locker or bin** for works in progress.
- **Use of every tool** you've been trained on: laser cutter, CNC router, mill and lathe, welders, 3D printers, the woodshop, and the electronics bench.
- **Discounts on paid classes** and first dibs on class seats.
- **A vote.** Members elect the board and approve the bylaws.
- **The community.** Members with decades of experience in machining, electronics, software, and woodworking who like answering questions.

## Dues

| Level | Monthly | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $50 | Full access for one adult. |
| **Supporter** | $100 | Full access, plus you're subsidizing a Thursday night or a scholarship. |
| **Scholarship** | $0 – reduced | Board-approved for students and anyone for whom dues are a barrier. Just ask. |

Dues are billed monthly through Givebutter and can be cancelled at any time. Dues are not tax-deductible to the extent you receive access in return; [donations](/donate/) are.

Cost should never be the reason someone doesn't join. If the standard rate is out of reach, [talk to us](/contact/) about a scholarship membership. It's a normal conversation and nobody will make it awkward.

## How to join

1. **Visit on a Thursday.** Get a tour, meet a few members, and make sure the shop is what you're looking for. Most people visit two or three times before joining.
2. **Sign the waiver and do a safety orientation.** Orientation takes about 30 minutes and covers shop rules, cleanup expectations, and where things are.
3. **Set up dues.** Use the button below; pick the level and "monthly".
4. **Get your credential.** A member of the membership team will set up door access, a locker, and your wiki and Discord accounts, and introduce you to the zone leads.

## Training

Some tools require training before you use them alone: the laser cutter, CNC router, Bridgeport mill, Clausing lathe, welders, and table saw. Training is discounted for members and is run by the zone lead on request or on a scheduled class night. Once you're signed off, it's recorded on the wiki and you're good to go.

## Rules, briefly

- Clean up after yourself. Leave every tool and bench the way you'd want to find it.
- Don't use a tool you haven't been trained on. Ask; someone will show you.
- Damage happens. Report it right away so it gets fixed, and nobody will be upset.
- Guests are welcome when you're present and responsible for them.
- Treat people well. See the [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/).

Full policies live on the [member wiki](https://wiki.comogadget.casa/).
