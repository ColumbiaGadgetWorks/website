Members teach hands-on classes in every part of the shop. These are free and open to everyone; no membership is needed. Seats are limited, so sign up to save yours. You get an email with the details, a reminder the day before, and a link to give your seat back if you cannot come.

The monthly classes run on the **first Tuesday of every month, 6 to 8 PM**, from November 2026 to October 2027, thanks to the City of Columbia Office of Cultural Affairs. Every class is also on the [calendar](/calendar/).
