Joining takes four steps and about ten minutes. Have a government-issued photo ID handy, since you will be asked for a picture of it. You can stop part way and come back: after the first step we email you a link that picks up where you left off.

If you have not visited yet, you are welcome to [come to a Thursday Open Hack Night](/visit/) first and see the shop before you sign anything. If dues are a barrier, [ask us about a scholarship membership](/contact/); it is a normal conversation.

{{< join >}}

## What happens with your information

Your name, email, and Discord username go into our membership records so we can set up your badge, your locker, and your wiki and Discord accounts. The signed waiver and agreement are kept with your membership record. The photo of your ID is used to confirm you are who you say you are, and only the membership team can see it. Dues are processed by Givebutter; we never see your card number. The full details are in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Prefer to do it in person?

That works too. Come by on a Thursday between 6 and 8 pm, and a member of the membership team will walk you through the same paperwork on the spot. See [membership](/membership/) for what members get and what dues cover.
