Zone bosses can ask to spend from any zone's budget; the board can ask for any zone or the General fund. You prove who you are with the email address you gave Columbia Gadget Works and the last two digits of your member code (for example 05).

Reimburse me is for something you already bought: attach the receipt, and file it within {{ $p.receiptDays | default 60 }} days. Request for purchase is for something you want the treasurer to buy: give the web link and price of each item (press + Add another item for more). Prices are an estimate; shipping, tax and other fees may be added when it is ordered. Pick the zone the money is for: its budget pays, and a reimbursement goes to you. The treasurer approves every request; over $100, or over what the zone has left, the president too; for a zone you are not the boss of, that zone's boss too. Nobody approves their own.
