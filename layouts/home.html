Columbia, Missouri · 501(c)(3) nonprofit makerspace

A community workshop where anyone can learn, build, and fix things.

Columbia Gadget Works gives people of all skill levels access to tools, workspace, and know-how: a laser cutter, CNC router, 3D printers, a metal shop, an electronics bench, and members who will show you how to use them.

Free Open Hack Night · {{ $p.openHackNight }} · No membership or experience required

Visit on a Thursday

Open Hack Night is free and open to everyone. Take a tour, meet members, use the basic tools, and bring a broken thing to fix. Walk in the door on Grand Avenue between 6 and 8 pm.

What to expect

Take a class

Members teach free and low-cost classes on soldering, metal casting, machining, and more. Most are announced a couple of weeks ahead on Discord and here.

Classes and workshops

Become a member

Members get 24/7 access to the shop, a project locker, discounts on classes, and a vote in how the space is run. Dues pay the rent and keep Thursday nights free.

What a Thursday can turn into

Members built a battle box out of steel, plywood, and polycarbonate, called it the Chamber of Robotic Aggression, and started putting robots in it. A combat robot touches nearly every zone in the building: welded frame, machined parts, printed wedges, and drive electronics from the bench.
